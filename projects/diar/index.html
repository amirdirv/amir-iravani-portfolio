Overview

The news outlet of Associazione DIAR. Persian-first, ten-language news site where readers submit, vote and discuss links while an autonomous agent drafts stories from 20 sources for human editors to approve from an installable PWA with push notifications. Built from zero: auth and roles, moderation, structured-data SEO, sitemaps and IndexNow.
